
# Appshare documentation

Appshare publishes your media and documents on your own website: photos, videos, music, PDFs and text files, organised in folders, at **your-name.appshare.site**.

- Upload your files with the **desktop application** for macOS or Windows
- Organise and publish them, and set up your website, in the **web admin**
- Invite your team: every account has users of its own
- Read your account's data as JSON with the **API**

### Where to start

- [Quick start](https://register.appshare.site/documentation#quick-start): create your account and publish your first folder
- Install the desktop application: [macOS](https://register.appshare.site/documentation#install-macos) or [Windows](https://register.appshare.site/documentation#install-windows)
- [Web application](https://register.appshare.site/documentation#web-application): your website and its admin
- [API guide](https://register.appshare.site/documentation#api-guide): your account's data from https requests

The state of our services is shown at [status.appshare.site](https://status.appshare.site).
