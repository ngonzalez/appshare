#### Choose your plan

Every account has a plan, paid monthly or yearly (two months free):

|                     | Pro                         | Business                    |
|:-------------------:|:---------------------------:|:---------------------------:|
| ***Storage***       | 5 GB                        | 10 GB                       |
| ***Users***         | up to 3                     | up to 10                    |
| ***Monthly***       | €29 / month                 | €39 / month                 |
| ***Yearly***        | €290 / year                 | €390 / year                 |

You can change your plan, your card or cancel at any time from the **Usage** tab of the web admin ("Manage billing").

#### Create your account

1. Go to [register.appshare.site](https://register.appshare.site) and choose your plan
2. Fill in your details and the address of your website: *your-name*.appshare.site
3. Check them, then pay securely with Stripe
4. Your account is created once the payment succeeds: validate your email address with the link we send you

#### Upload your files

Install the desktop application for [macOS](https://register.appshare.site/documentation#install-macos) or [Windows](https://register.appshare.site/documentation#install-windows), sign in, and upload a folder: each folder you upload becomes a folder of your website.

Files of up to 5000 MiB each are uploaded straight to our storage: images, audio, video, PDF and text files. Your plan sets how much your files may weigh in all.

#### Publish your folders

A folder you upload is **created**: only your account's users see it. **Publish** it to show it on your website, now or between two dates, or **archive** it to take it down. Do it from the desktop application or the web admin.

#### Invite your team

From your account page in the desktop application, invite someone by email: they receive a link to join your account, valid for 7 days, and choose their name and password. Your plan sets how many users your account may have.
