#### Your website

Your website is at **your-name.appshare.site**, the address you chose when you created your account: anyone can see its published folders, search them and play their files. It works with Google Chrome, Apple Safari, Microsoft Edge and Firefox.

#### Sign-in to the admin

Click the admin button at the bottom of your website, then sign in with your email address and password.

<br />

![img](https://api.appshare.site/attachment/screenshot-2026-10-25-at-2-47902e84)

#### The admin

The admin's tabs set up your website:

- **Themes**: the logos and the colors of your site, for its light and dark themes
- **Meta Tags**: the tags search engines and social networks read on your pages
- **Links**: the links in the footer of your pages
- **Export**: your folders, files and events as CSV or Excel files
- **Stats**: charts of your pages' visits and downloads
- **Uploads**: what was uploaded, day by day
- **Usage**: your plan, how much storage your files take, your users, and your subscription
- **Credentials**: your website's addresses, your API token and the command line tool's settings

<br />

![img](https://api.appshare.site/attachment/screenshot-2026-10-25-at-2-44652e63)

#### Your folders and their publication

Change your folders' names and tags, and their publication: published (always, or between two dates), archived, or created (seen by your account's users only).

<br />

![img](https://api.appshare.site/attachment/screenshot-2026-10-25-at-2-471fe0e3)

#### Your plan and your subscription

The **Usage** tab shows your plan, the storage your files take and your users. "Manage billing" opens your subscription on Stripe: change your plan or your card, read your invoices, or cancel.
